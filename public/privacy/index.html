Privacy, in plain language

Exam Bridge works without an account and keeps planner content in your browser.

What stays on your device

Your plan name, topics, confidence choices, prerequisites, practice references, and theme stay in browser storage. They are not sent to Exam Bridge.

Clearing site data removes them. Export a JSON backup first if you want a portable copy.

Demo storage

The demo uses separate keys beginning with demo:exam-bridge:. It never reads or changes your real plan.

Reset demo restores the sample. Start for real deletes the demo keys.

Network and analytics

The app loads no third-party scripts, fonts, advertisements, or behavioral analytics.

The planner does not send your plan to an external service.

Standard hosting logs may contain an IP address, browser information, requested path, and timestamp. We do not use these logs to profile you.

Your choices

Export your plan as CSV or JSON at any time. Use Start over to delete the current plan.

Remove Exam Bridge site data in browser settings to erase plans and theme choices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
