Privacy, in plain language

Your study plan belongs to you. Exam Bridge is built to work without an account and keeps planner content in your browser.

What stays on your device

Your plan name, syllabus topics, confidence choices, prerequisites, practice references, theme, and optional license token are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to Exam Bridge. Clearing site data removes them; use the JSON backup first if you want a portable copy.

License checks

If you restore an existing Exam Bridge Plus license, your token is sent to the Sociobot billing API solely to verify whether that license is active. The verification result and timestamp are cached locally for up to one day. If hosted checkout returns, Sociobot/Dodo will process checkout data under its own privacy terms. Exam Bridge never receives card details.

Network and analytics

The app loads no third-party scripts, fonts, advertisements, or behavioral analytics. Standard hosting logs may temporarily contain an IP address, browser information, requested path, and timestamp for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to profile you.

Your choices

Export your plan as CSV or JSON at any time. Use “Start over” to delete the current local plan. Remove all Exam Bridge site data in your browser settings to erase the plan, theme, and license cache.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
